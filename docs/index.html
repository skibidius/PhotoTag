What it does

◎

Full-resolution capture

Shoots at the sensor's maximum resolution and stamps without downscaling.

⌖

Location watermark

Coordinates in DD, DMS, DDM, UTM or MGRS, plus bearing, altitude, accuracy, address and a counter.

▤

Projects & export

Group shots by site or client and export a project to ZIP — originals, watermarked, or both.

ⓘ

EXIF metadata

GPS, altitude and direction are also written into the file's EXIF.

↻

Background queue

Keep shooting while photos are watermarked and saved in the background.

✦

Offline & bilingual

GPS and geocoding work offline. English and Russian interface.
